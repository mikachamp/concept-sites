Good to know. Every page carries a concept banner and a noindex tag. Contact details are replaced with "[... to confirm]" markers, photos showing identifiable people are replaced with placeholders, and forms send nothing. Three.js, GSAP and fonts load from public CDNs, so open these online. The README has the full story.
